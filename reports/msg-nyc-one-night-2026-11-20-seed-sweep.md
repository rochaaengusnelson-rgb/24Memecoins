## Takeaways

1. **A bigger seed buys the owner a much larger share of the coin.** $500 buys 8.8% of supply; $5,000 buys 50.5%. pump.fun prices rise as the curve sells, so each extra dollar buys fewer tokens, but the owner still holds a growing stake.
2. **The relative upside from attendance shrinks.** A full house adds the same $19,200 of value, which is +313.0% on a $6,134 launch but only +103.9% on $18,472.
3. **Selling mid-show (S3): the percentage falls but the dollars rise.** ROI goes +361.9% → +287.6% → +159.4% → +115.5% while the dollar gain goes $1,816 → $2,881 → $4,785 → $5,776. A bigger bag dumped into the same crowd extracts more in total.
4. **Holding into the burn (S1) costs the owner more as the seed grows** (−$201 → −$698 → −$2,660 → −$4,594) **and cushions the crowd** (micro-trader P&L −$4,844 → −$4,454 → −$3,642 → −$3,181). The burned tokens leave their SOL in the curve, backing everyone else's tokens. Creator fees don't scale with the seed.
5. **Who pays when the owner sells mid-show:** micro-trader P&L goes −$6,054 → −$6,326 → −$7,011 → −$7,068 across seed amounts. The doors sniper (W1) goes $4,551 → $4,245 → $3,408 → $2,843, because a bigger seed means W1 buys in at a higher price right after the owner. With leftovers valued at what the curve can actually pay, every dollar the owner and winning whales take out comes from other traders or the owner's own seed, less fees.
6. **At $5,000, the curve fills in some runs** (122 of 1000). pump.fun then migrates the coin to PumpSwap; trading after that isn't modeled, so those runs understate later trading.

## Assumptions

- Same trader model as `npm run market`: made-up but plausible behavior parameters, so compare across seed amounts rather than reading absolute values as forecasts.
- Owner cost = seed + 0.0092 SOL account rent. Tokens still held at window close are valued together at what selling all of them into the curve would return, split pro rata (no paper gains). Burned tokens are gone, which raises everyone else's share.
- SOL/USD constant at $185; attendance is the modeled profile in `events/msg-nyc-one-night.json`. Not financial advice.
